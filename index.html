Swindler's Roulette

Rig the wheel. Pay the debt. Don't get caught.

You're a swindler buried in loan-shark debt, and the only way out runs through an underground casino's roulette wheel. You've been tampering with it.

Pay off the debt every night for 15 nights. Bolt mods onto the wheel's 12 pockets, stack charms and dirty tricks into a build, and keep the pit boss from noticing what you're doing.

  • Rig the wheel

    31 wheel mods snap onto the pockets, up to three per pocket. When the ball lands, they fire in order.

  • Build a con

    37 charms and 28 consumables across seven families. Collect two, four or six of a family for set bonuses.

  • Watch the heat

    Every trick raises suspicion. At 50 the pit boss starts watching pockets. At 100 you're dragged to the back room.

  • 15 nights, 5 bosses

    The nightly debt climbs from 10 to 270K. The casino's regulars run the boss nights, and Hades owns the house.

Made with generative AI tools for code, pixel art, music and sound. No real money is involved: in-game currency only.

About

Orvel Games makes roguelite indie games. We like runs short enough to replay and builds that get out of hand. Each game starts from one rule we want to break, and we keep breaking it until it's fun.

We build with Godot and an AI-assisted pipeline for code, art and sound, and publish our games ourselves, starting on itch.io.
